From first wobble to full glide

Every session follows the same path. Nobody goes into the woods until the instructor is happy, and the route is tailored to your group.

  1. 1

    Meet your Segway

    A short introduction to the Segway X2, then the practice area to get your balance.

  2. 2

    The training course

    Slalom round the cones and build the confidence for rougher ground.

  3. 3

    Over the bridge

    Into 15 acres of woodland on undulating, fully off-road tracks.

  4. 4

    The see-saw

    One last test of control on the way back. Can you balance on it?
