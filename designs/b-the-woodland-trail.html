Before you come

Good to know

Age and weight

Age 10+ for the Woodland Glide, 14+ for Multi Activity. Riders 45kg to 177kg (7.1 to 18½ stone).

What to wear

Comfortable clothes for the weather and secure shoes: trainers or walking boots. No flip-flops, sandals or heels.

Weather

We run all year. If rain, snow or ice make it unsafe we will rebook you.

Times

Tours from 9.30am to 4pm in winter and 9.30am to 6pm in summer.

Bringing someone?

1 or 2 spectators per rider, and plenty to do at Blakemere Village.

Training

Full training is included. Nobody goes into the woods until the instructor is happy.
