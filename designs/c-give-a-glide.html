How a voucher works

1

You buy it

An e-voucher to print or email with your own message, or a gift card sent first class the same day.

2

They pick a date

Any day of the week, any time in the next 12 months.

3

They glide

Bring the voucher on the day. We do the training and provide all the safety kit.

Weekends and holidays go fast; at busy times it can be worth booking up to 8 weeks ahead.
